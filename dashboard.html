    Yield on Cost & Current Yield

    Trailing-12-month, per-unit-normalised: each payment is divided by the units held on that exact payout date, so a lot bought or sold mid-year no longer distorts the rate. The trailing window itself adapts to each holding's own payout frequency, rather than a fixed day count, so a quarterly, semi-annual or annual payer is never cut off mid-cycle. Yield on cost applies that trailing rate to today's average cost; current yield applies it to today's price.
